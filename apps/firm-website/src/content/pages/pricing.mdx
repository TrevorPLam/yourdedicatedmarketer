## Common Questions

### Do I own my website?
Yes. You own 100% of your website, content, and assets. We build it for you—you keep it.

### Are there any hidden fees?
No. All pricing is transparent and disclosed upfront. The only ongoing costs are the services you explicitly sign up for.

### What if I need changes after launch?
Minor content edits are included in the Hosting & Care Plan. Larger changes can be quoted as separate projects.

### Can I cancel my retainer?
Yes. Monthly retainers are month-to-month with no long-term contract required. We believe in earning your business every month.

### What's included in the Hosting & Care Plan?
Hosting, security, updates, backups, and up to 30 minutes of content edits per month. See the full details above.

### Do I have to sign a long-term contract?
No. We don't require long-term contracts for any of our services. We prefer to earn your continued business through results.
