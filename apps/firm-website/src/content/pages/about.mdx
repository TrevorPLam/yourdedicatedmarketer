# About Your Dedicated Marketer

We help DFW service businesses get online fast and affordably, using AI-assisted workflows to deliver professional websites and marketing support that would otherwise be out of reach.

## Our Story

Your Dedicated Marketer was founded on a simple observation: too many local service businesses in the Dallas–Fort Worth area are either overcharged by traditional agencies or stuck with outdated websites that don't generate leads. Market research shows custom small business websites typically cost $2,000-$8,000+ in DFW, putting professional web presence out of reach for many local businesses.

We saw an opportunity to change this. By leveraging AI-assisted coding and design workflows, we compress build timelines and deliver comparable output at a fraction of traditional agency costs. This allows us to offer professional websites at prices that undercut the market while maintaining custom quality and fast turnaround times.

## Our Method

Our approach is different by design. We use AI-assisted production to handle technical heavy lifting—coding, layout optimization, and content structure—while maintaining human oversight for strategy, messaging, and quality control. This hybrid model lets us deliver 7-14 day turnaround times without sacrificing the personal attention your business deserves.

We focus on what matters: lead generation, local visibility, and measurable results. Every website we build includes mobile-responsive design, SEO foundations, and conversion-focused elements designed to turn visitors into customers.

## Local Connection

Based in Euless, Texas, we're part of the DFW community. We understand the local market because we live in it. Our service area spans the entire Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, from Arlington to Plano, Denton to Cedar Hill. We know the challenges local trades, clinics, and service businesses face because we work with them every day.

Being local means we can meet in person, understand your specific market context, and provide support that's actually available when you need it—not through a call center in another time zone.

## Our Vision

We're working to become the DFW area's go-to marketing partner for small service businesses—known for making professional web presence accessible to every local business, regardless of budget. We believe every local business deserves a website that works as hard as they do.

## Credibility & Trust

We're a Single-Member LLC registered in Texas, committed to transparent pricing and honest communication. No hidden fees, no long-term contracts required, and clear deliverables upfront. During our launch phase, we're offering select free and discounted website builds to build our portfolio and demonstrate our capabilities—proof that we stand behind our work.

Our growth roadmap is intentional: we're starting with website design as our anchor service, with plans to expand our marketing offerings as we grow. This focused approach ensures we deliver excellence in what we do best before taking on more.
