# Plumbing Business Website

## The Situation

A family-owned plumbing business serving the Dallas-Fort Worth area had been relying on word-of-mouth referrals and a basic Google Business Profile. Their existing website was outdated, not mobile-optimized, and failed to capture emergency service calls. Competitors with better mobile presence were capturing most of the urgent plumbing searches in their service area.

## The Challenge

The business needed a website that could convert emergency searches into immediate phone calls. Key challenges included:

- Most searches happened on mobile devices during plumbing emergencies
- The old site loaded slowly and didn't display prominently on mobile
- No clear call-to-action for emergency services
- Service areas weren't clearly defined, leading to calls from outside their coverage zone
- Trust signals (licensing, insurance, guarantees) weren't visible above the fold

## The Approach

We built a mobile-first website designed specifically for emergency service capture:

- **Click-to-call prominence**: Phone buttons placed above the fold on every page, with sticky mobile navigation
- **Service area clarity**: Dedicated pages for "Emergency Plumbing," "Water Heater Installation," and "AC Repair" with clear service area maps
- **Fast load times**: Optimized images and minimal JavaScript to ensure sub-2-second load times on mobile networks
- **Trust signals front and center**: Licensing, insurance, and guarantees displayed prominently on the homepage
- **Google Business Profile integration**: Connected the site to their GBP for consistent NAP data and review display

## The Outcome

This is a proof-of-concept demo showcasing our approach to home service websites. The design prioritizes mobile optimization and emergency conversion paths, which are critical for trades businesses. While this is not a live client site, the structure and features are based on industry best practices for capturing emergency service calls.

The demo demonstrates how a mobile-first, click-to-call optimized website can address the unique needs of plumbing and home service businesses in the DFW market.
