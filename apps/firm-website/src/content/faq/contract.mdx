No, you don't have to sign a long-term contract for website builds. Website projects are one-time engagements with clear deliverables and payment terms. Monthly retainers like the Hosting & Care Plan and marketing services require 30 days notice to cancel but don't have long-term lock-in periods. We believe in earning your business every month through value, not contracts.

## Website Build Projects

**No Long-Term Contract Required**
- One-time project with defined scope and deliverables
- Payment terms: 50% deposit, 50% on launch (Core package)
- 40/30/30 payment schedule for Growth packages
- Project completes when deliverables are met
- You own the site outright upon completion

## Monthly Services

**Hosting & Care Plan** ($99/month)
- Month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel
- Required for all hosted sites
- No long-term commitment beyond hosting needs
- We'll help you transition if you leave

**Marketing Retainers** (Local SEO, Paid Ads, Email/SMS)
- Month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel
- No minimum term requirements
- Cancel anytime if not seeing value
- We want you to stay because it's working, not because you're locked in

## Bundling Discounts

If you commit to multiple services or annual prepayment, we offer discounts:
- 2 retainer bundles: 10% off combined monthly fee
- 3+ retainer bundles: 15% off combined monthly fee
- Annual prepay on web build + retainer: 1 month free

These are optional—discounts for commitment, not requirements.

## Why No Long-Term Contracts?

We're confident in the value we provide. If we're not delivering results, you shouldn't be stuck. Our business model is built on long-term client relationships earned through performance, not contract lock-in. We'd rather earn your renewal every month than trap you in a contract you regret.
