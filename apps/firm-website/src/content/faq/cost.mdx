## What Influences Price?

Page count, custom features, integrations, and content needs all affect the final cost. We provide transparent quotes upfront so there are no surprises. The Core package fits most established small businesses, while Growth is for companies ready to scale with marketing automation and advanced SEO.

## Monthly Costs

The Hosting & Care Plan is $99/month and required for all sites. This covers hosting, security updates, daily backups, and minor content edits (up to 30 minutes/month). Optional retainers like Local SEO ($399/month) and Paid Ads Management ($750/month) are available but not required.
