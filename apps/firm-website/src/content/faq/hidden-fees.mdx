No, there are no hidden fees. All pricing is transparent and communicated upfront. The only ongoing cost is the $99/month Hosting & Care Plan, which is clearly stated as required. Optional services like Local SEO ($399/month) and Paid Ads Management ($750/month) are optional and only added with your explicit approval. Third-party costs like domain registration (~$15/year) are passed through at cost.

## One-Time Build Costs

**Website Build**: Package price as quoted
- Launch Case-Study: Free or $250
- Core Site: $1,000
- Growth Site: $2,500+

**Add-Ons** (if selected during build):
- Copywriting: $250-$500
- Branding/logo refresh: $200
- On-page SEO setup: $150
- Google Business Profile setup: $100
- Analytics setup: $100
- Graphic design: $75 per item

## Ongoing Monthly Costs

**Required**:
- Hosting & Care Plan: $99/month

**Optional** (only if you choose them):
- Local Visibility Bundle: $399/month
- Lead Acceleration (Paid Ads): $750/month management fee
- Retention Starter (Email/SMS): $399/month

## Third-Party Costs (Passed Through)

- Domain registration: ~$15/year
- Premium plugins (if needed): varies by plugin
- Stock photos (if purchased): varies by license
- Ad spend (for paid campaigns): billed directly to you

## Payment Terms

- 50% deposit for Core sites, balance on launch
- 40/30/30 payment schedule for Growth sites
- Monthly retainers billed in advance
- All terms agreed upon before work begins

## Clear Communication

We document everything in writing before starting work. If a project scope changes, we provide a revised quote for your approval before proceeding. No surprise invoices—ever.
