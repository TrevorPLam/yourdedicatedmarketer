Yes, you own your website completely. You own all content, design, and the domain name. We build your site on your behalf, but it's your asset. The only ongoing requirement is the Hosting & Care Plan ($99/month) to keep the site hosted, secure, and updated—but you're free to move your site to another host at any time with proper notice.

## What You Own

- **Domain Name**: Registered in your name or business name
- **Content**: All copy, images, and text you provide or we create for you
- **Design**: Custom design work created specifically for your business
- **Code**: The website codebase itself

## What Happens If You Leave

If you decide to move your site to another host or developer, we'll provide a full export of your site files, database, and content. There's a small administrative fee for the export process, but you retain full ownership. We believe in building long-term relationships through value, not lock-in.

## Hosting & Care Plan

The Hosting & Care Plan is a service, not a ownership restriction. It covers hosting, security monitoring, daily backups, WordPress/core updates, and minor content edits. You can cancel this plan at any time with 30 days notice—we'll help you transition your site to a new host if needed.

## Third-Party Services

Some third-party services (domain registration, premium plugins, stock photo licenses) may have their own terms. We use services that allow you to maintain control, and we'll set up accounts in your name whenever possible.
