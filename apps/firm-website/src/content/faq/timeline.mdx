Most small business websites are completed in 7-14 days using our AI-assisted build process. Launch Case-Study sites typically finish in 7 days, while Core and Growth sites take 10-14 days depending on complexity and revision rounds. This is significantly faster than traditional agencies, which often require 4-8 weeks.

## Build Process Timeline

**Discovery Call** (Day 1)
- 30-minute call to understand your business, goals, and target audience
- Discuss package selection and any custom requirements

**Content Strategy** (Day 2-3)
- Site structure and sitemap planning
- Key messaging and copy direction
- Asset collection (logos, images, existing content)

**Design & Build** (Day 4-8)
- AI-assisted design and development
- Mobile-responsive implementation
- SEO foundation setup
- Integration of contact forms and analytics

**Review & Refine** (Day 9-12)
- Client review and feedback
- Up to 3 revision rounds (Core and Growth packages)
- Final polish and testing

**Launch** (Day 13-14)
- Go-live deployment
- Domain and hosting setup
- Post-launch checks and support

## What Can Extend Timeline?

Complex custom features, third-party integrations, extensive content creation, or delayed client feedback can extend the timeline. We work efficiently and communicate clearly to keep projects on track. The 7-14 day estimate assumes timely client participation and standard requirements.

## Rush Options

For urgent projects, we can sometimes accelerate the timeline depending on current capacity. Discuss your deadline during the discovery call and we'll assess feasibility.
