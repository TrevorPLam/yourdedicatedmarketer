The Hosting & Care Plan at $99/month includes secure hosting, daily automated backups, WordPress/core updates, security monitoring, and up to 30 minutes of minor content edits per month. It's required for all websites we build to ensure they stay secure, updated, and performing well. This pricing sits at the accessible end of the standard small-business maintenance range ($95-$195/month market average).

## Hosting & Performance

- **Secure Hosting**: Reliable, fast hosting optimized for WordPress
- **SSL Certificate**: HTTPS security for all sites
- **CDN Integration**: Content delivery network for faster page loads
- **Uptime Monitoring**: We track and respond to downtime quickly

## Security & Updates

- **Core Updates**: WordPress and plugin updates applied regularly
- **Security Monitoring**: Malware scanning and threat detection
- **Daily Backups**: Automated daily backups with 30-day retention
- **Restoration Support**: Quick recovery if issues arise

## Content & Support

- **Minor Edits**: Up to 30 minutes per month for text/image updates
- **Priority Support**: Faster response times for care plan clients
- **Performance Monitoring**: Regular checks on site speed and health
- **Plugin Management**: Keeping plugins updated and compatible

## What's Not Included

- Major redesign work or new page creation
- Advanced SEO work beyond foundation
- Third-party service costs (domain renewal, premium plugins)
- Content creation (blog writing, copywriting)

## Why It's Required

We require the care plan because WordPress sites without regular maintenance become security risks and performance liabilities. Updates, backups, and security monitoring are essential—not optional. The $99/month pricing is competitive while ensuring we can provide proper ongoing support.

## Market Comparison

Small business website maintenance typically runs $20-$600+ per month. Basic DIY plans are $20-$50/month but offer minimal support. Standard small business plans are $95-$195/month with updates, security, and minor edits. Our $99/month plan sits at the accessible end of this standard tier with full-service coverage.
