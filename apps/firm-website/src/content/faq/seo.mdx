Your website will be built with SEO best practices from day one, but ranking takes time and ongoing effort. We include local SEO foundation in all packages—proper structure, meta tags, and optimization for local searches. Meaningful rankings typically develop over 3-6 months with consistent content and local optimization efforts.

## What's Included in Every Site

- **Technical SEO**: Fast loading, mobile optimization, proper URL structure
- **On-Page SEO**: Meta titles, descriptions, heading hierarchy, image alt tags
- **Local SEO Foundation**: Google Business Profile connection, local schema markup
- **Analytics Setup**: GA4 installation to track performance

## Realistic Timeline Expectations

**New Websites**: 3-6 months to see meaningful rankings
- Google indexes new sites quickly, but trust builds over time
- Local keywords often rank faster than competitive broad terms
- Consistent updates and content accelerate progress

**Competitive Markets**: May take 6-12 months
- Highly competitive keywords require more authority and backlinks
- Local SEO typically shows results faster than organic SEO

## What Improves Rankings

- Regular content updates (blog posts, service pages)
- Google Business Profile optimization and reviews
- Local citations and directory listings
- Quality backlinks from relevant sites
- User engagement signals (time on site, low bounce rate)
