Core and Growth packages include 3 revision rounds during the build process. **Core and Growth Packages**: 3 revision rounds
- Review rounds after design, content, and final build

**Launch Case-Study Package**: 1 revision round
- Single review round to ensure satisfaction
- Focus on essential adjustments only

## Post-Launch Support

**Hosting & Care Plan** ($99/month)

- Security updates and backups included
- Priority support response time

**Beyond Included Time**

- Project quotes for larger updates

## What Counts as Minor Edits?

Minor edits are quick changes that don't require design or structural work: updating phone numbers, changing service descriptions, swapping photos, fixing typos, adding blog posts. Major changes like new pages, redesign work, or feature additions are quoted separately.

## Long-Term Partnership

Many clients prefer to retain us for ongoing updates and improvements. We can discuss a custom arrangement if you need regular monthly work beyond the included 30 minutes. Our goal is to be your long-term marketing partner, not just a one-time build vendor.
