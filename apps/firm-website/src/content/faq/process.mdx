Our website build process follows a clear 6-step approach: discovery call, content strategy, design & build, review & refine, launch, and ongoing support. The entire process typically takes 7-14 days using our AI-assisted workflow. We communicate clearly at each stage so you always know what to expect next.

## Step 1: Discovery Call (Day 1)

A 30-minute call to understand your business, goals, and target audience. We'll discuss:
- Your business and what makes it unique
- Your target customers and their needs
- Your website goals (lead generation, bookings, information)
- Package selection and any custom requirements
- Timeline and next steps

## Step 2: Content Strategy (Day 2-3)

We plan your site structure and messaging:
- Site map and page organization
- Key messaging and value propositions
- Content needs assessment (copy, images, existing assets)
- Asset collection from you (logos, photos, existing content)

## Step 3: Design & Build (Day 4-8)

Our AI-assisted process creates your custom site:
- Design concept tailored to your brand and industry
- Mobile-responsive implementation
- Content integration and optimization
- Contact forms, analytics, and SEO setup
- Testing across devices and browsers

## Step 4: Review & Refine (Day 9-12)

You review the site and we refine based on feedback:
- Live preview for your review
- Up to 3 revision rounds (Core and Growth packages)
- We implement your feedback within scope
- Final polish and quality assurance

## Step 5: Launch (Day 13-14)

We go live and ensure everything works:
- Domain connection and hosting setup
- Go-live deployment
- Post-launch testing and checks
- Analytics verification
- Handoff documentation

## Step 6: Ongoing Support

The Hosting & Care Plan keeps your site running:
- Security updates and monitoring
- Daily backups
- Minor content edits (up to 30 minutes/month)
- Priority support when you need help

## Your Role in the Process

We handle the technical work, but your input matters:
- Provide information about your business and customers
- Share existing assets (logos, photos, content)
- Review and provide feedback during revision rounds
- Be available for questions and decisions

We keep you informed at each stage with clear communication and timelines.
