# Your Dedicated Marketer — Website Content & Sitemap Plan

## 1. Overview & Strategic Foundation

### Purpose of This Document

This document defines the full sitemap, page-by-page copy direction, and SEO/AEO strategy for the Your Dedicated Marketer website. It translates the firm's three core differentiators — discoverability, credibility through design, and unique solutions per business — into a concrete site structure and content plan.

### Guiding Principles

- **Lean navigation, deep structure:** Primary navigation stays to 5-7 items; SEO volume comes from deeper, interlinked sub-pages rather than a cluttered top nav.
- **One job per page:** Every page has one primary conversion goal, one fallback path for visitors not ready to convert, and a clear next step.
- **Programmatic-style SEO with real substance:** Industry and service pages follow a repeatable template (service × industry) but each must contain genuinely distinct content — real pain points and specifics, not swapped placeholders.
- **Demo sites replace testimonials:** Since no clients/reviews exist yet, proof-of-concept demo builds substitute as the trust and credibility mechanism.
- **AEO-ready structure:** Headings phrased as natural questions, answered directly in the first 40-60 words, to be extractable by both traditional search and AI-driven answer engines.
- **Transparency as trust signal:** Published pricing, clear process steps, and defined timelines substitute for testimonials and long tenure.

### Website Design Service Page (Anchor Page — 800-1,000+ words)

**H1:** Website Design & Development for DFW Service Businesses

**Intro (100-150 words):** Name the audience and pain point directly (DFW trades/clinic/personal-service owner losing customers to competitors with better online presence), then pivot to the promise of fast, affordable, custom-built sites that convert visitors into calls and bookings.

**The Solution (150-200 words):** Explain the AI-assisted coding + real framework approach (not a drag-and-drop builder), framed as the reason agency-quality work is possible at a fraction of typical DFW pricing ($2,000-$8,000+ elsewhere).

**Packages Section:** Comparison table of Launch Case-Study / Core / Growth tiers — price, page count, included features.

**Process Section (4-6 steps):** Discovery Call → Sitemap & Wireframe → Design & Copy → Build & Integration → QA & Launch → 30-Day Polish Window.

**Questions We're Asked (AEO format):**

- How much does a website cost for a small business in DFW?
- How long does it take to build a website?
- Do I own my website once it's built?
- What if I need changes after launch?

**Proof Section:** Links to 2-3 relevant demo sites, industry-matched where possible.

**Final CTA:** "See what a custom-built website could do for your business — book your free consultation today." Secondary link to Pricing page.

## 4. SEO & AEO Implementation Rules

**Site-wide technical rules:**

- One H1 per page with logical H2/H3 hierarchy
- Short, descriptive URLs
- Consistent NAP (name/address/phone) across site and Google Business Profile
- Local business schema site-wide; service schema on individual service pages

**AEO-specific rules:**

- Headings phrased as natural questions where applicable (e.g., "How much does a small business website cost in DFW?")
- Direct answers delivered in the first 40-60 words of each answer block, expanded afterward
- FAQ sections on every service and industry page, not just the dedicated FAQ hub

**Internal linking structure:**

- Every Industry page links to its matching Demo page and vice versa
- Every Service page links to relevant Demo pages as proof substitutes
- Industries hub and Services hub link downward into all respective sub-pages

**UX rules applied site-wide:**

- Sticky header for navigation access on long pages
- Breadcrumbs given the site's deep structure
- CTA buttons meet 44x44 pixel minimum tap target for mobile
- CTAs repeat after every major content section, each stating a specific promise (e.g., "Book a Free Consultation") rather than generic text
