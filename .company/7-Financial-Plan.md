### Break-Even Analysis

With finalized pricing and verified overhead costs, break-even can now be calculated.

**Fixed monthly costs:** ~$28/month (Google Workspace $7 + Cursor/Windsurf $20 + domain ~$1.25)

**Variable cost per website build:**
- Cursor/Windsurf subscription is a fixed cost (not per-build)
- Hosting pass-through: ~$5-15/month per client site (if reselling hosting as part of Care Plan)
- Domain for client: ~$15/year (if included in build)
- Payment processing: 2.9% + $0.60 per card transaction (Wave)
- Founder time: not monetized as a variable cost since no personal income requirement exists initially

**Estimated variable cost per Core build ($1,000):** ~$35-50 (payment processing ~$29 + domain ~$1.25 + misc ~$5-20)
**Contribution margin per Core build:** ~$950-965

**Break-even point:** $28 monthly overhead ÷ $950 contribution margin = **0.03 Core builds per month**

In practical terms, **a single paid Core-tier build covers roughly 34 months of baseline overhead.** Overhead is not the binding constraint. The business is viable from the first paid project. The real question is revenue growth rate, not break-even survival.

**With insurance added (if a client requires it):** ~$81/month additional ($42 GL + $39 E&O for marketing consultants in Texas) [1800insurance](https://www.1800insurance.com/guides/texas-consulting-insurance-requirements) [moneygeek](https://www.moneygeek.com/insurance/business/best-professional-liability-insurance-texas/). Break-even becomes $109/month ÷ $950 = 0.11 builds/month — still less than one build every 9 months.
