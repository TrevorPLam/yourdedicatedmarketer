# Your Dedicated Marketer — Master Business Document

## Section 1: Executive Summary

**Business Name:** Your Dedicated Marketer

**Legal Structure:** Single-Member LLC (Certificate of Formation filed with Texas Secretary of State; $300 one-time filing fee)

**Location:** Euless, Texas

**Name Status:** Fully verified — "Your Dedicated Marketer" is cleared across Texas SOSDirect, Texas Comptroller, USPTO trademark database, domain registration, and primary social media platforms.

### Mission Statement

To help DFW service businesses get online fast and affordably, using AI-assisted workflows to deliver professional websites and marketing support that would otherwise be out of reach.

### Vision Statement

To become the DFW area's go-to marketing partner for small service businesses — known for making professional web presence accessible to every local business, regardless of budget.

### Business Concept

Your Dedicated Marketer is a web-first marketing firm launching in the Dallas–Fort Worth (DFW) area, built around website design as its primary service, supported by complementary marketing services sold individually or bundled. The firm differentiates on price by using AI-assisted coding and design workflows to reduce production time and cost, allowing it to significantly undercut prevailing local market rates for website design, including offering select free builds to early clients in exchange for testimonials, case studies, and recurring service enrollment.

### Strategic Rationale

Standard small-business website design in the DFW market typically ranges from approximately $1,500 to $10,000 depending on scope and complexity, with many local agencies quoting $3,000–$8,000 for a custom lead-generation site. AI-assisted production allows Your Dedicated Marketer to compress build timelines and deliver comparable output at a fraction of this cost, creating a pricing advantage during the launch phase that would be difficult for traditionally-staffed agencies to match without sacrificing margin.

### Target Market

The initial target market is local service-based businesses in the DFW area (trades, clinics, personal and professional services) that need a credible, lead-generating website and are underserved or overcharged by traditional agencies. This segment was selected based on observed market pricing gaps and the firm's ability to compete aggressively on cost while building a local reputation and referral base.

### Revenue Model

Revenue is structured across four tiers with finalized pricing:

1. **Anchor service** — website design and development: Launch Case-Study Site (free for builds 1-3, $250 for builds 4-5), Core Small-Biz Site ($1,000), Growth Site ($2,500+). All priced aggressively below DFW market rates of $2,000-$8,000+ for comparable work.
2. **Build-time add-ons** — copywriting ($250-$500), branding/logo refresh ($200), on-page SEO setup ($150), Google Business Profile setup ($100), analytics setup ($100), basic graphic design ($75/item). Sold individually or bundled at 15% discount with a web build.
3. **Recurring retainers** — Hosting & Care Plan ($99/month), Local Visibility Bundle ($399/month), Lead Acceleration/Paid Ads ($750/month), Retention Starter/Email & SMS ($399/month). Multi-retainer bundles discounted 10-15%. This tier is the firm's primary long-term profit driver.
4. **Future/scale-later services** — e-commerce development, video, PR, and marketing automation, to be introduced as capacity and client demand grow.

Full pricing rationale and market benchmarks are documented in the Services & Packaging section.

### Growth Roadmap (Summary)

The firm is planned to evolve in three phases over 12–18 months: an initial aggressive-pricing launch phase (Months 0-3) focused on completing 3-5 case-study builds for portfolio and testimonial building, a transitional phase (Months 3-9) raising the minimum project price floor to $1,000 and requiring recurring-service attachment, and a maturity phase (Months 9-18) where website design becomes one of several services rather than the primary offer, supported by retainer clients.
