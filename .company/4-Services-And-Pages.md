## Section 4: Services & Packaging

### Service Structure Overview

Services are organized into four tiers: the core anchor service (website design), build-time add-ons, recurring monthly retainers, and future/scale-later services. This structure reflects the standard approach used by successful small agencies of leading with one service done well before expanding into a broader menu.

### Tier 1: Anchor Service — Website Design & Development

This is the firm's primary offer and pricing differentiator, delivered through an AI-assisted build process targeting a 7–14 day turnaround.

| Package | Price | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Launch Case-Study Site (builds 1-3) | Free | 1–3 pages, mobile-responsive, contact form, basic on-page SEO; limited to first 3 DFW businesses; requires Hosting & Care Plan enrollment ($99/month) |
| Launch Case-Study Site (builds 4-5) | $250 setup | Same scope as above; partial cost recovery for last 2 case-study slots; still requires Hosting & Care Plan enrollment |
| Core Small-Biz Site | $1,000 | 5–7 pages, custom copywriting, analytics setup, local SEO foundation |
| Growth Site | $2,500+ | Everything in Core, plus landing pages for ads, schema markup, CRM/email integration, 90-day content roadmap |

**Pricing rationale:** DFW market rates for custom small business websites range from $2,000-$8,000+ at agencies, with fully custom design running $8,000-$25,000. [intrglobal](https://intrglobal.com/blog/dallas-web-design-costs.html) [saltechsystems](https://saltechsystems.com/web-design-cost-for-small-business-dallas-tx/) The Core tier at $1,000 undercuts even template-based agency work ($750-$1,500) while delivering more value through custom copywriting and local SEO. The Growth tier at $2,500 sits at the bottom of the custom agency range while including features (landing pages, schema, CRM integration) that agencies typically charge $5,000+ for.
